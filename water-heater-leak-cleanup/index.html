A water heater leak cleanup starts after the heater has stopped adding water to the floor. In Littleton, CO, most tanks sit in a basement, a utility closet or a garage corner, which means the water often runs under walls and into finished rooms before anyone notices. A residential tank holds tens of gallons, and a failed seam can release it in a short burst.

Tanks fail in a handful of ways. Sediment and corrosion eat through the steel over the years, fittings and supply connectors loosen or rust, and the temperature and pressure relief valve can discharge when pressure climbs. Each one puts hot or warm water on the floor, and each one needs a different repair from a plumber.

When a water heater leak is an emergency

Treat it as urgent when water is pooling, spreading toward carpet, or coming from the top or the seam of the tank. A steady drip at a fitting still soaks the subfloor every hour it runs.

Safety steps for the first hour

  1. Stop the supply: close the cold water valve on the pipe feeding the heater. If it will not turn, close the main shut-off for the house.
  2. Cut the power or gas: switch off the breaker for an electric heater, or turn the gas control to off. Do not stand in water to reach a panel.
  3. Mind the scald risk: water from the tank and relief valve can be hot enough to burn, so keep children and pets out and wear closed shoes.
  4. Call a plumber for the heater and call us for the water. If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 first.

Do not touch wet electrical gear

If water has reached an outlet, a power strip or the breaker panel, stay out of the room and leave the power decisions to an electrician. Water and live circuits do not mix, and a wet slab carries current further than you expect.

Insurance and the permit for a replacement heater

Colorado Division of Insurance advisories state that burst pipes and the resulting water damage are generally covered under homeowners and renters policies. A gradual leak that went unnoticed for months is treated differently, and many policies exclude it. Contact your insurer as soon as you can, take reasonable steps to stop more damage, and get their instructions before repairs begin. Ask your insurer about deadlines.

Mistakes to avoid with a leaking water heater

  • Leaving the power or gas on while the tank is empty, which can burn out an electric element.
  • Mopping and walking away, since water left in the wall base keeps soaking upward.
  • Hiring anyone for the swap without asking about the permit.

Littleton, CO: shut-off, permits and policy wording

Denver Water's cold weather tips place the main shut-off valve for most single-family homes in the basement or crawl space on a street-facing wall. Find it today and tag it, because a tank leak at night is easier to stop when you do not search for the valve.

The City of Littleton Permit Center at Littleton Center, 2255 W. Berry Ave, says a permit is required to install, alter, repair or replace any electrical, gas, mechanical or plumbing system. A water heater swap touches plumbing and often gas, so ask the Permit Center at 303-795-3751 whether your replacement needs one, and ask your plumber who pulls it.

Colorado Division of Insurance consumer advisories say burst pipes and their resulting damage are generally covered while gradual leaks often are not. Keep the failed tank, the plumber's note and your photos, since they show whether the failure was sudden.
