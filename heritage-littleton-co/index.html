Heritage is a residential area of Littleton, Colorado. The City of Littleton's neighborhoods map shows it as one of the city's named areas, alongside Downtown, Goddard, South, Southwest and Trailmark. The map is a planning tool and the city says it is not for general public use, so we do not draw street-level borders for Heritage.

Instead, we confirm coverage by address when you call. That also avoids mixing Heritage up with Heritage Hills, a separate unincorporated community that uses a Littleton mailing address.

What real estate guides say about the housing

Real estate guides describe Heritage as a mostly single-family area with medium to large homes, many built between 1970 and 1999, and some from 1940 to 1969. One guide describes rows of single-family homes built in the late 20th century. Neighborhood data sites also report apartment complexes and condos in the area.

These are third-party descriptions, not city records. They tell us the mix is varied, so we ask what kind of home you have before suggesting a plan. We make no assumption about basements.

Questions we ask on the first call

1

What kind of home is it?

A single-family house, a condo, a townhome or an apartment each calls for a different first move.

2

Where did the water start?

A supply line, an appliance, the roof, a unit above or a drain. The source decides the water category.

3

What is wet?

Floors, walls, ceilings, cabinets and contents. We ask which floor level is affected.

4

Who else needs to know?

Condo and apartment residents should tell the association or landlord right away, since shared pipes and walls may be involved.

Safety first

Do not walk through floodwater or touch the breaker panel while standing in water. Do not run household fans or a vacuum on sewage. If you smell gas, leave and call 911.

How we handle the dry-out

We locate the wet materials with moisture meters and thermal imaging, extract standing water, then set air movers and dehumidifiers. We check readings on each visit and remove materials only when drying cannot reach them. Some materials cannot be saved, and we say so when readings show it.
