A roof leak lets water in from the top of the house, so the damage travels downward. It wets attic insulation first, then the ceiling drywall, then walls, floors and furniture below. By the time a stain appears, the water has often spread well beyond it.

We do not repair roofs. We remove the water and dry what it soaked in your Littleton, CO home, and a roofer fixes the entry point.

How water gets through a roof here

The common entry points are worn or lifted shingles, cracked flashing around chimneys and skylights, plumbing and exhaust vents, and valleys where two roof planes meet. In winter, ice dams add a second route. Snow melts from the warm upper roof, refreezes at the cold eave, and the trapped meltwater is pushed back under shingles.

Leaks also arrive after heavy rain, hail or wind that moves shingles.

When a roof leak is an emergency

A slow stain is a problem. A bulging ceiling, dripping light fixture or steady stream is an emergency. Drywall holds a surprising amount of water, and a ceiling full of it can drop without warning.

Call right away if water is running down a wall, the ceiling is sagging or you see water near a light or outlet. Move out of the room first, since a failing ceiling can injure someone.

Safety steps for the first hour

  1. Keep everyone out from under a sagging or dripping ceiling.
  2. Turn off the breaker for any room where water is near lights, fans or outlets, if you can reach the panel safely.
  3. Place buckets and move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the drip path.
  4. Photograph the damage, call your insurer, then call our 24/7 line.

Tarps and roof access are roofer work

Climbing onto a wet or snowy roof is dangerous, and temporary tarping is done by roofing crews. We do not tarp or repair roofs. Ask a roofer about a temporary cover while we handle the water inside.

What changes the cost of a roof leak loss

  • How many rooms sit under the leak, and whether it reached a second floor.
  • The amount of insulation and ceiling board that must come out.
  • Whether the attic needs drying as well as the living space.
  • Whether the leak is still active, which can delay drying until the roof is covered.

We explain the scope and the price before we start, and we separate drying items from rebuild items so the paperwork is easy to read. Drying follows the approach on our water mitigation page, and the rebuild of ceilings and paint is covered by water damage repair.

Insurance and permits for a roof leak

The Colorado Division of Insurance says to contact your insurer as soon as possible and to take steps to prevent further damage, while getting the insurer's instructions before repairs. Ask whether the leak is treated as sudden damage or wear.

Prevention and mistakes to avoid

  • Do not paint over a ceiling stain until the area is measured dry.
  • Do not leave wet attic insulation in place, since it keeps feeding the ceiling below.
  • Do not start repairs before talking to your insurer.

Littleton, CO: snow, melt and roof repair rules

NWS Boulder lists Denver's normal annual precipitation at 14.48 inches and says the western and southwestern Denver suburbs average about 65 to 70 inches of snow a season. These are metro numbers, not Littleton readings. After each large melt, look at attic insulation and the ceilings beneath it, because ice dams force meltwater under shingles.

The City of Littleton Permit Center, at 2255 W. Berry Ave, requires a permit to install, alter, repair or replace electrical, gas, mechanical or plumbing systems. If a leak damaged a light circuit, ask the Permit Center at 303-795-3751 whether the repair needs a permit.

Colorado DORA says roofers are not state-licensed, and Littleton requires a contractor license and proof of liability insurance to pull permits. Ask any roofer for its Littleton license before signing.
